Privacy Policy

Last Bastion: Turret Defense · Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Last Bastion iOS game ("the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. We have no accounts, no login and no servers of our own. We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, stars, gems and upgrades are saved on your iPhone. The app also keeps a local log of gameplay events (for example, "level started" or "ad watched") to help with balancing during development. This log is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Tracking and personalised ads

When the app first shows ads, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier isn't shared and ads aren't personalised using it. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In regions where consent is required (such as the EU/EEA and the UK), Google's consent form asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings → Privacy options in the game. Buying "No Ads" removes interstitial ads.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your rights

Since we keep no data on our own servers, we hold no personal information to give you, correct or delete. You can delete your on-device data by deleting the app. You can also reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings, and make requests about third-party data directly to the providers above. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us and we'll help you use them.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
